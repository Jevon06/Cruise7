Cruise Travel Guide

Cruising is a great way to explore new destinations, enjoy entertainment, and relax while traveling. This guide will help you prepare for your trip.

What to Know Before Your Cruise

  • Pack clothing suitable for your destination.
  • Bring your travel documents and identification.
  • Research your cruise line and destinations.
  • Plan activities and excursions before departure.

Responsive Design Reflection

  1. The most significant layout issue I discovered was that the website needed a more consistent content area and better spacing. The original page also needed improvements to its image markup and stylesheet connection.
  2. I used box-sizing, max-width, margin, padding, and flexible widths to organize the content. I also used max-width: 100% and height: auto to keep images from overflowing their containers.
  3. At wider screen widths, the content has more room and the page looks less crowded. At narrow widths, the content uses the available space, and the navigation links stack vertically.
  4. Testing helped me understand why websites must adapt to different devices. It also showed me the importance of checking images, text wrapping, spacing, and horizontal scrolling.
  5. My next improvement would be to create a more attractive photo gallery and add better mobile navigation. I would also continue testing the website on different devices and browsers.
